
This is a webapp which I plan to turn into a full native app. It was originally requested by my flatmate — they wanted something simple to organise our flat chores. I sort of went overboard.

What started as a chore roster turned into a full webapp where flatmates can create or join a flat, add chores with configurable frequencies, and assign those chores to select individuals, a group, or the whole flat. The app then distributes and rotates them evenly — daily, weekly, or monthly.

Shortly after, we added a Shopping List feature so flatmates can log their shopping and expenses and divvy out the finances between them, whether that is the whole flat or just a select few.

## Getting Access

Contact me for the link.

### Inviting Flatmates

Once your flat is set up, go to **House > Invite Flatmates**. You can either:
- Send an email invite to your flatmate email address, or
- Share them the flat code directly — they can enter it when they sign up or from the join screen

### Chore List

Under **House > Chore List**, the flat can add chores as they see fit. For each chore you choose:
- Whether it repeats **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly**
- An optional description of how it should be completed
- Which flatmates it applies to — the whole flat, or specific people

These chores will then appear in the **Home** tab, assigned to whoever turn it is. Tap a card to expand it, tick the box, and call it a day.

### Shopping and Expenses

The **Shopping** tab is for splitting finances. To log an item:
1. Add the item name and its cost
2. In the next panel, select who paid for it
3. Below that, select who you would like to split it between

The app then computes each person share and holds it there, waiting for the bill to be settled.
